# The money trail in Argentina's illegal gambling investigations
This repository analyzed the information contained in 10 court cases about illegal gambling: the investigated websites, its cashiers, reports about transfers and the role of minors
# Here you can read this investigative data driven story

# Findings based on court cases
An investigated network of at least 39 online casinos and 91 cashiers. Two of them are minors, according to the information in bank documents.
The amount of money received by two accounts under investigation for allegedly receiving funds from illegal gambling, and to whom it was transferred. 
266 accounts under minors' name appear transferring money to one of the investigated accounts. Natural pdf was used to extract data from the files.
# Findings based on searchings outside court cases
Scrapping Telegram public channels and its messages allowed to detect, by using regex, 24 urls which deliver users to online gambling. That sites are different from the ones mentioned in court cases.
Applying browser automation through them was a way of knowing which documents were requested in order to create a user in those sites.
# Visualization
Graphs were made with Observable. 
Illustrations were downloaded from SVG repo and then customized in illustrator.
Everything they show is based on the information obtained analyzing court cases, the Telegram messages and the gambling sites.
# Limitation
Identities involved in this story were preserved because some of them are minors.
